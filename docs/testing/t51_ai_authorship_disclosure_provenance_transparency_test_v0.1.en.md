# T51 — AI Authorship Disclosure / Provenance Transparency Test v0.1

Status: **PASS — AI AUTHORSHIP DISCLOSURE CONTRACT DEFINED / PROVENANCE BOUNDARIES PRESERVED**

## 1. Purpose
T51 defines and tests how every generated Research Note discloses AI participation in its generation and structuring while preserving distinctions among AI-generated, AI-structured, human-authored, human-edited, source-derived content, and epistemic provenance.

The objective is transparency, not AI detection. Disclosure must not try to make prose “look AI-generated”; it must make AI authorship and generation provenance explicitly inspectable.

## 2. Governing Principle
Every Research Note generated through the Research Note Engine must contain explicit AI authorship disclosure.

Recommended fixed disclosure:

> **AI-generated / AI-structured Research Note**
>
> This document is a Research Note generated and structured with AI through the Research Note Engine. AI-generated Claims or interpretations have their own Evidence/Verification states; document storage or generation alone does not verify factual accuracy. The human retains final epistemic judgment and review responsibility.

Wording may be revised as a project-level presentation decision, but the disclosure must remain explicit and machine-detectable.

## 3. Why Explicit Disclosure Is Required
AI authorship should not be inferred from writing style, vocabulary, formatting, verbosity, model-specific phrasing, or statistical AI-text detection. These are unreliable authorship indicators.

Authoritative disclosure consists of provenance metadata and an explicit document marker.

## 4. Three-Layer Disclosure Model

### Layer A — Visible Document Marker
Every generated note displays:

**AI-generated / AI-structured Research Note**

This is a presentation-layer marker.

### Layer B — Generation Provenance
Every generated note retains this chain:

**Conversation → Research Object → Claim / Evidence → Transformation → SARA → Research Note**

This indicates how the artifact was produced.

### Layer C — Structured Disclosure Metadata
Use the existing `confession_report` field for structured disclosure.

Minimum information:
- AI participation: YES;
- generation/structuring system: Research Note Engine;
- human review status;
- AI-generated vs source-derived vs human-edited content where materially distinguishable;
- epistemic disclaimer;
- generation provenance reference.

No new epistemic entity is required.

## 5. Authorship Attribution Model

### A1 — AI-generated
Text or analytical structure directly generated by AI during note creation.

### A2 — AI-structured
Underlying information originated from conversation/user/source, but AI performed its organization, classification, decomposition, or synthesis.

### A3 — Human-authored
Content explicitly supplied by the user/human and retained as authored content.

### A4 — Human-edited
AI-generated or AI-structured content materially modified by a human.

### A5 — Source-derived
Content representing a source, quotation, data, or authorial Claim with preserved provenance.

These are authorship/provenance labels, not epistemic states.

## 6. Critical Boundary: “AI-assisted” vs “AI-authored”
If AI generated the note's prose and analytical structure, disclose the note as AI-generated/AI-structured even when the user supplied the underlying ideas.

If the user subsequently rewrites substantial portions, do not imply that every sentence was generated by AI.

**AI participation ≠ AI authorship of every token.**

The disclosure concerns artifact generation history; local attribution can identify material human edits.

## 7. Revision / Extension / New Preservation

### REVISION
Keep disclosure attached to the revised artifact. Add human-edit provenance if the human materially edits it.

### EXTENSION
New AI-generated sections retain AI provenance. Human additions are separately attributable when materially distinguishable.

### NEW
The new note receives its own AI disclosure and generation provenance.

### NO_CHANGE
No new artifact is generated; existing disclosure remains unchanged.

### BLOCKED
A non-persisted AI-generated candidate must still carry the disclosure. Persistence failure must not remove or weaken it.

## 8. Branch / Merge Preservation

### Branch
Each generated branch retains provenance. A human-authored branch must not be mislabeled as entirely AI-generated just because the parent note was AI-generated.

### Merge
Record source-branch provenance, AI transformation/synthesis provenance where applicable, and human contributions where materially identifiable.

Merge does not alter epistemic state.

## 9. D9 Preservation
An AI-generated/structured D9 note must disclose AI participation and preserve:

**Source Notes → Source Claims/Evidence → Integration Transformation → Integrated Claims → D9 Research Note**

D9 PROMOTED does not alter authorship attribution. PROMOTED ≠ AI verification and PROMOTED ≠ human authorship.

## 10. Content Attribution Rule
Do not label all content AI-generated solely because AI rendered the final document.

Examples:
- user's explicit statement → A3 Human-authored;
- source author's proposition → A5 Source-derived;
- AI synthesis → A1 AI-generated;
- AI categorization of user material → A2 AI-structured;
- human rewrite of an AI paragraph → A4 Human-edited.

When local attribution cannot be determined reliably, retain the artifact-level disclosure and mark local attribution unresolved rather than inventing precision.

## 11. Controlled Fixture Set
- **T51-01 — Fully AI-Generated Note:** user supplies research question; AI creates complete note. Expected marker, populated confession_report, provenance, and A1 where local attribution exists. **PASS**
- **T51-02 — User-Supplied Material, AI-Structured:** disclose artifact; user material A3, AI organization A2; do not claim AI originated the ideas. **PASS**
- **T51-03 — Human Revision:** retain AI disclosure; mark materially human-edited sections A4 where applicable; do not claim all final wording was AI-generated. **PASS**
- **T51-04 — Source-Derived Content:** disclose AI participation; source proposition A5; preserve provenance; do not label source content AI-originated. **PASS**
- **T51-05 — Revision with New Evidence:** preserve AI provenance and Revision Event; preserve new-analysis attribution and source Evidence A5; handle epistemic state independently. **PASS**
- **T51-06 — Branch:** each branch has provenance; branch identity retained; no branch becomes authoritative merely by being AI-generated. **PASS**
- **T51-07 — Merge:** record merge Transformation, source branch provenance, AI synthesis A1/A2, and known human contributions. **PASS**
- **T51-08 — D9:** disclose AI participation and retain source-note provenance, integration Transformation, integrated-Claim provenance, and independent D9 state. **PASS**
- **T51-09 — Non-Persisted Candidate:** locked Notion target; candidate retains disclosure, persistence = BLOCKED, no saved-status claim. **PASS**
- **T51-10 — Ambiguous Local Attribution:** mixed content with uncertain sentence-level authorship; retain artifact disclosure, mark local attribution unresolved, do not fabricate attribution. **PASS**

## 12. Failure Conditions
- **F1 — Missing disclosure:** generated note has no explicit disclosure. FAIL.
- **F2 — Style-based disclosure:** system infers disclosure from prose style instead of provenance. FAIL.
- **F3 — Attribution inflation:** all content labeled AI-generated despite known human/source provenance. FAIL.
- **F4 — Attribution erasure:** human contributions labeled AI-generated without basis. FAIL.
- **F5 — Provenance loss after revision:** human editing erases AI-generation history. FAIL.
- **F6 — D9 provenance collapse:** integrated content loses source-note/transformation provenance. FAIL.
- **F7 — Persistence dependence:** disclosure appears only after successful Notion persistence. FAIL.
- **F8 — Epistemic conflation:** AI authorship label treated as Evidence, Verification, or Claim state. FAIL.

## 13. Display Contract
Recommended rendering:

> **AI-generated / AI-structured Research Note**
>
> Generation/structuring: Research Note Engine  
> AI participation: YES  
> Human review: [NOT REVIEWED / IN REVIEW / REVIEWED]  
> Generation provenance: [reference]

Visual placement may vary by Document Type, but disclosure remains visible and machine-detectable.

## 14. Structured Disclosure Contract
Recommended fields:
- ai_participation = YES
- generation_system = Research Note Engine
- artifact_attribution = AI_GENERATED / AI_STRUCTURED
- human_review = PENDING / IN_PROGRESS / COMPLETED
- human_editing = NONE / MATERIAL / UNKNOWN
- source_derived_content = YES / NO / UNKNOWN
- provenance_reference = [reference]
- disclosure_version = v0.1

These are disclosure/provenance fields, not epistemic states.

## 15. Architecture Decision
**RETAIN CURRENT CORE MODEL.**

AI authorship disclosure uses the presentation marker, provenance, and existing `confession_report`. No new Research Object, Claim, Evidence, Verification, Document Type, or Notion database is required.

## 16. Result
**T51 = PASS — DISCLOSURE / PROVENANCE CONTRACT VALIDATED WITH CONTROLLED FIXTURES**

The system can disclose AI participation while distinguishing AI-generated, AI-structured, human-authored, human-edited, and source-derived material.

Artifact-level disclosure is mandatory even when local sentence-level attribution is uncertain.

Those require context-specific external policy or human/legal review.
