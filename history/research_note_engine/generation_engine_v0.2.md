# Research Note Generation Engine v0.2 (가칭)

Status: HISTORICAL / SUPERSEDED

## Revised Pipeline
Research Object
→ Initial / Provisional Classification
→ Candidate Document Type
→ Evidence Mapping
→ SARA
→ Final Reclassification / Split / Merge
→ Final Note

## New Decisions
- RETAIN
- RECLASSIFY
- SPLIT
- MERGE
- DOWNGRADE
- UPGRADE

## Evidence Mapping
Source → Evidence Role → Claim

Roles:
SUPPORT, CONTRADICT, CONTEXT, ILLUSTRATE, BACKGROUND.

## Epistemic States
VERIFIED, SUPPORTED, PARTIALLY_SUPPORTED, UNCERTAIN, HYPOTHESIS, INTERPRETATION, UNVERIFIED, CONTRADICTED.

## Provenance
Taxonomy Provenance, Evidence Provenance, Generation Provenance.

## Boundary
Conversation is a source session; research notes are independent knowledge objects. One conversation may yield multiple notes.

## Historical Limitation
T2/T3 showed that interpretive provenance required explicit source-layer separation. This was incorporated conditionally in v0.3.
