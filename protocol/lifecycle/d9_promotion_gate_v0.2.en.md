# D9 Candidate → D9 Promotion Gate v0.2 (provisional project proposal)

Status: **PROJECT PROPOSAL / OPERATIONAL DRAFT**

## Purpose

D9 is promoted only when an integrated structure is more than a well-organized summary and passes gates for relations, a higher-order question, structure, claims, evidence, and network-level verification.

## Gates

### G1 — Relation
The target notes have substantive, evidence-relevant relations.

### G2 — Higher-order Question
A synthesis question exists that cannot be answered by simply repeating one source note.

### G3 — Novel Structure
The candidate produces an integrable structure, explanation, model, or relation network.

### G4 — Claim Decomposition
The integrated model is decomposed into independently truth-evaluable component claims and relations.

### G5 — Evidence Mapping
Claims and relations have explicit evidence mappings and evidence roles.

### G6 — Network-level SARA
Verification → Revision → Re-verification is applied at the network level.

G6 may be:
- PASS
- PARTIAL PASS / REVISION REQUIRED

## Revision Loop

G6
→ Revision
→ Re-verification
→ G5 Re-check
→ G6 Re-check
→ Promotion Re-entry

Revision types:
SCOPE_NARROWING
CLAIM_DOWNGRADE
CLAIM_REFORMULATION
RELATION_REFORMULATION
CONDITION_ADDITION
CLAIM_SPLIT
CLAIM_MERGE
EVIDENCE_REMAP
RESEARCH_GAP_EXTRACTION
DOCUMENT_TYPE_RECLASSIFICATION

## Promotion Re-entry

Recheck only affected claims, relations, evidence, G5, and G6 unless the higher-order question or integration itself changed; in that case, recheck G2 and subsequent gates.

## Promotion Rule

D9 PROMOTED requires G1–G6 all to be PASS.

D9 PROMOTED ≠ Model VERIFIED.

A promoted synthesis may still contain claims with HYPOTHESIS, UNCERTAIN, or other non-verified states.

## Failure Routes

Depending on the failure:
- RELATE
- EXTEND
- REFINE
- D8 literature/evidence synthesis
- Research Gap
- revise
- reject

## Promotion Record

Record:
gate result, rationale, affected claims, evidence mapping, SARA state, revision history, promotion decision, date/version.

The human retains final decision authority.
