## 2. Separate Axes

Do not create new taxonomies when an established axis or metadata field already expresses the distinction.

Separate axes:
- Question Operator
- Research Design
- Method
- Evidence/Source
- Knowledge Domain
- R&D Orientation
- Claim/Epistemic Status
- Knowledge Product
- Document Type
- Review Type
- Synthesis Operation

Purpose ≠ Method. Purpose ≠ Document Type. Claim ≠ Evidence.

## 3. Primary / Secondary Purpose

Primary Purpose: maximum 1.

Secondary Purpose: zero or more.

If a secondary purpose has an independent research question, evidence structure, and conclusion, create a separate Research Object rather than hiding the independence in metadata.
