# D9 Candidate → D9 Promotion Gate v0.2 (가칭)

Status: **PROJECT PROPOSAL / OPERATIONAL DRAFT**

## Purpose

D9 is promoted only when an integrated structure is more than a well-organized summary and passes relation, higher-order question, structure, claim, evidence, and network-level verification gates.

## Gates

### G1 Relation
Target notes have substantive, evidence-relevant relations.

### G2 Higher-order Question
A synthesis question exists that cannot be answered by simply repeating one source note.

### G3 Novel Structure
The candidate produces an integrable structure, explanation, model, or relation network.

### G4 Claim Decomposition
The integrated model is decomposed into independently truth-evaluable component claims and relations.

### G5 Evidence Mapping
Claims/relations have explicit evidence mappings and evidence roles.

### G6 Network-level SARA
Verification → Revision → Re-verification is applied at network level.

G6 can be:
- PASS
- PARTIAL PASS / REVISION REQUIRED

## Revision Loop

G6
→ Revision
→ Re-verification
→ G5 Re-check
→ G6 Re-check
→ Promotion Re-entry

Revision types:
SCOPE_NARROWING
CLAIM_DOWNGRADE
CLAIM_REFORMULATION
RELATION_REFORMULATION
CONDITION_ADDITION
CLAIM_SPLIT
CLAIM_MERGE
EVIDENCE_REMAP
RESEARCH_GAP_EXTRACTION
DOCUMENT_TYPE_RECLASSIFICATION

## Promotion Re-entry

Only affected claims/relations/evidence/G5/G6 need rechecking unless the higher-order question or integration itself changed; then G2+ is rechecked.

## Promotion Rule

D9 PROMOTED requires G1–G6 all PASS.

D9 PROMOTED ≠ Model VERIFIED.

A promoted synthesis may still contain claims with HYPOTHESIS, UNCERTAIN, or other non-verified states.

## Failure Routes

Depending on failure:
- RELATE
- EXTEND
- REFINE
- D8 literature/evidence synthesis
- Research Gap
- revise
- reject

## Promotion Record

Record:
gate result, rationale, affected claims, evidence mapping, SARA state, revision history, promotion decision, date/version.

Human remains final decision authority.
