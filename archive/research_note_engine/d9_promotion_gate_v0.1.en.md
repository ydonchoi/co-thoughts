# D9 Candidate → D9 Promotion Gate v0.1 (provisional project term)

Status: HISTORICAL / SUPERSEDED BY v0.2

## Gates
- G1 Relation
- G2 Higher-order Question
- G3 Novel Structure
- G4 Claim Decomposition
- G5 Evidence Mapping
- G6 Network-level SARA

D9 PROMOTED requires G1–G6 to PASS.

## Key Rule
D9 PROMOTED ≠ Model VERIFIED.

A candidate with a partial G5/G6 result remains a candidate and cannot be promoted.

## Failure Routes
RELATE, EXTEND, REFINE, D8, Research Gap, revise, reject.

## Later Revision
v0.2 added an explicit Revision Loop and Promotion Re-entry:
G6 → Revision → Re-verification → G5 Re-check → G6 Re-check → Promotion Re-entry.
