# Research Note Taxonomy v0.1 (가칭)

Status: HISTORICAL / RECONSTRUCTED / SUPERSEDED

> This document reconstructs the project's first explicit research-purpose taxonomy from the recorded design state. It is not a byte-for-byte historical snapshot.

## Research Purpose

The initial taxonomy separated research purpose into five broad outcomes:

- R1 탐색 — discovering or clarifying phenomena, concepts, structures, problems, hypotheses/models
- R2 기술 — describing characteristics, composition/classification, distribution, and change
- R3 설명 — explaining relationships, influence/causation, mechanisms, processes/systems
- R4 예측 — predicting states, trends, outcomes, and conditional scenarios
- R5 평가 — evaluating effects, performance, impacts, processes, and improvement/decision alternatives

## Initial Design Principle

Research Purpose was treated as a classification of the epistemic outcome sought by an inquiry, not as a method, document format, or evidence type.

## Known Limitation

At this stage, the taxonomy was intentionally coarse. Question structure, research design, method, evidence, document type, and other axes had not yet been fully separated into an explicit axis-audit architecture.
