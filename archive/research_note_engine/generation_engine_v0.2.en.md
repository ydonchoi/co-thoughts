# Research Note Generation Engine v0.2 (provisional project term)

Status: HISTORICAL / SUPERSEDED

## Revised Pipeline
Research Object  
→ Initial / Provisional Classification  
→ Candidate Document Type  
→ Evidence Mapping  
→ SARA  
→ Final Reclassification / Split / Merge  
→ Final Note

## New Decisions
- RETAIN
- RECLASSIFY
- SPLIT
- MERGE
- DOWNGRADE
- UPGRADE

## Evidence Mapping
Source → Evidence Role → Claim

Roles:
SUPPORT, CONTRADICT, CONTEXT, ILLUSTRATE, BACKGROUND.

## Epistemic States
VERIFIED, SUPPORTED, PARTIALLY_SUPPORTED, UNCERTAIN, HYPOTHESIS, INTERPRETATION, UNVERIFIED, CONTRADICTED.

## Provenance
Taxonomy Provenance, Evidence Provenance, Generation Provenance.

## Boundary
A conversation is a source session; research notes are independent knowledge objects. One conversation may yield multiple notes.

## Historical Limitation
T2/T3 showed that interpretive provenance required explicit separation of source layers. This was conditionally incorporated in v0.3.
